The shared inbox is haunted. Here's the exorcism.

Every small business inbox is the same: 200 unread, half of them asking a question that's answered on your website, three of them genuinely urgent, and no way to know which three without reading all 200.

You don't need an assistant. You need the 80% of replies that are routine — hours, pricing, "do you do X", reschedules — drafted before you've had coffee, and the 20% that need a human flagged in red.

What's inside

Refund demands, legal-ish language, genuinely angry customers: what the AI should never answer for you, and how to spot it.
