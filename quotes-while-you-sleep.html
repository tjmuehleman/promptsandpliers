PLAYBOOK 02 · FREE FOR THE FOUNDING 100

Voice memo at the job site. Clean estimate by breakfast.

The job isn't the hard part. The hard part is 9pm at the kitchen table, typing up what you already said out loud at the site that afternoon — and the three quotes you owe people are why you haven't sent any.

Slow quotes lose jobs. The contractor who responds in two hours beats the better contractor who responds in five days. This playbook turns the ramble you recorded in the truck into a structured, priced, professional estimate — your numbers, your terms, none of your typing.

What's inside

The voice-memo workflow

What to actually say into your phone (a 90-second checklist) so the AI has everything it needs on the first pass.

The estimate prompt

Turns your transcript into line items, quantities, labor, and terms — formatted to paste into your invoicing tool or send as-is.

Margin protection rules

The AI never sets your prices. You feed it your rates; the playbook shows how to keep it from 'helpfully' rounding things down.
